# Storylines considered

1. **A release record, earned** (selected): one developer's acceptance plan fails, the correction is rerun, and both reports remain available. Real UI fills the frame while short captions carry the task. Best fit for a Check-in because the evidence is visible.
2. **Evidence first**: open on the saved report, then trace each claim back through its observation. More suitable for an audit audience than a first product introduction.
3. **Two interfaces, one question**: a diagram follows EVM and native account identities. More conceptual, and it would overemphasize capabilities whose signed deployment proof is still pending.
