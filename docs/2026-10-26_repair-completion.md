## Browser-store migration

No storage key changed. The studio store `revivecheck.studio.v2` keeps its shape: sessions gain `history[].reason` on archived receipts and readbacks attach to the matching transaction; existing sessions load unchanged. The projects store `revivecheck.projects.v1` keeps its shape: `release.owner`, `release.releaseId` and `release.declared` are new optional fields that read as null on existing records until a bundle import or an adoption sets them; `sessionFor` overlays the project's current plan on the session when it is read. The request owner of `src/studio-requests.js` is in-memory per page load; nothing of it is stored. A browser that used the site before this round continues with its projects, sessions and receipts as they were.
